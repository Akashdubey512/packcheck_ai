## 1. Executive Summary & Calibration Semantics

The Phase 5 confidence subsystem measures multi-factor heuristic confidence across OCR text quality, candidate feature extraction, value normalization, image quality, and spatial consistency.

> [!CRITICAL]
> **Strict Distinction Between Heuristic Score & Calibrated Probability**:
> - Composite scores generated by heuristics are explicitly labeled as `confidence_type: UNCALIBRATED_SCORE`.
> - Uncalibrated scores are **never** presented as true calibrated probabilities.
> - When ground-truth probability calibration datasets are unavailable, `calibrated_probability` returns `null` and `calibration_status` returns `CALIBRATION_UNAVAILABLE`.

## 3. Calibration Scaling Architecture (Platt & Isotonic Regression)

When ground truth annotations are provided:
1. **Platt Scaling**: Logistic regression fitted on raw composite scores $s \in [0, 1]$:
   $$\sigma(A \cdot s + B) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-(A \cdot s + B)}}$$
2. **Isotonic Regression**: Non-parametric monotonic fitting for non-linear confidence mapping.
3. **Evaluation Protocol**: Expected Calibration Error (ECE) computed using 10 equal-width probability bins.
